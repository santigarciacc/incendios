# Release v1.3.0 — end-August 2026 prospective snapshot

This release supports the manuscript **“Forecasting annual counts of large wildfires in Spain: separating extreme-heat signals, prevention expenditure and time-series memory.”**

## Scientific freeze

- Historical model fitting and model selection are frozen through **2025**.
- The **2026 wildfire season is not used to refit, tune or select models**.
- This release archives the prospective Spanish snapshot through **31 August 2026**: 44 provisional large wildfires and 264,656 ha of forest area affected.
- The frozen ARIMA(1,0,0) forecast for 2026 remains 17.44 large wildfires, with a 90% prediction interval of 4.62–59.54.
- Developments reported after 31 August 2026, including subsequent European wildfire events, are intentionally excluded from this release and belong to later prospective follow-up.

## Contents

- harmonised annual Spanish modelling series (2005–2025);
- ARIMA/ARIMAX benchmark code and lag sensitivity;
- negative-binomial frequentist and Bayesian count models;
- rolling-origin and external validation outputs;
- PCA and predictor-redundancy analyses;
- prospective 2026 frozen forecast and dated provisional snapshots;
- manuscript-supporting derived tables and figures.
